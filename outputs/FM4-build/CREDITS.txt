Motorsport DX: credits

Motorsport DX runs Forza Motorsport 4 (Xbox 360), recompiled for Windows from the player's own
discs. It would not exist without the people and projects below.

The ReXGlue SDK - https://github.com/rexglue/rexglue-sdk
  The recompiler and the runtime that Motorsport DX forks. Its README credits Tom (crack), the
  project founder; Loreaxe (Linux), mystixor (Windows), Carlos Estrague (mrcmunir, Linux and
  ARM64), sanjay900 (Linux and SDL) and Roxxsen (CI/CD) as contributors; Graine25 and Toby for
  project support; and everyone in the ReXGlue community.

Xenia - https://xenia.jp - https://github.com/xenia-project/xenia
  Ben Vanik and the Xenia project contributors. Their Xbox 360 emulation laid the ground that
  ReXGlue and this runtime stand on: the kernel, the GPU and the audio. Xenia Canary
  (https://github.com/xenia-canary/xenia-canary): the optional FM4 shadow workaround follows its
  game patches, and the vector dot products follow its arithmetic.

XenonRecomp - https://github.com/hedge-dev/XenonRecomp - hedge-dev and contributors
  It pioneered the modern approach to Xbox 360 static recompilation; much of ReXGlue's code
  analysis and instruction translation is based on its work.

rexdex's recompiler - https://github.com/rexdex/recompiler
  The original Xbox 360 static recompiler, which ReXGlue names as an inspiration.

XenosRecomp and Unleashed Recompiled - https://github.com/hedge-dev/XenosRecomp -
https://github.com/hedge-dev/UnleashedRecomp - hedge-dev
  Their shader recompiler and their port of a game at the Direct3D level showed the way for
  this project's shader mods and its native render path. They were read for ideas; this
  project uses no code of them.

The Xbox 360 homebrew, modding and reverse-engineering communities, whose research is the
shared knowledge that projects like this build on.

The tools that build it: LLVM and Clang, CMake and Ninja, Python, Git, Microsoft's Visual
Studio build tools, the Windows SDK with its shader compiler (FXC), and the DirectX Shader
Compiler.

Turn 10 Studios, who made Forza Motorsport 4.

The code of other projects in the program files: SDL 3 (Sam Lantinga and contributors), Dear
ImGui (Omar Cornut), FFmpeg and libmspack, AMD FidelityFX (CAS, FSR 1, and FSR 3.1 with -Fsr
on), Microsoft's GameInput header and loader and d3dx12.h, Polyphony Digital's GT7 tone mapping
sample (SIGGRAPH 2025, the tone curve of the gt7 look), {fmt}, spdlog, toml++, xxHash, SIMD
Everywhere, o1heap, CLI11, UTF8-CPP, JSON for Modern C++, stb_image and the small libraries that
ReXGlue keeps from Xenia. Microsoft's DirectX Shader Compiler compiles the native path's
shaders; the launcher fetches it on the player's computer.
